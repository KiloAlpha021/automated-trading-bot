## Boundaries and dependencies

The existing domain package owns these contracts; section 7's conceptual
`contracts.decision` owner is mapped to `automated_trading_bot.domain.decision`
without renaming the accepted package. M1.6 builds on the existing identifier,
Quantity, OrderSide and UTC Timestamp contracts. M1.5 event behavior is preserved;
decisions are not repurposed as events or broker commands.

Validity is the half-open interval [issued_at, expires_at). Exact quantity
matching is intentionally conservative; approval is not a transferable cap.
A nonblank explicit version identity can represent an inapplicable model or
feature; a missing identity is rejected. Version/evidence identities are opaque
strings, not evidence verification services. Quantity finiteness/positivity is
enforced at this new action boundary without changing the established Quantity
primitive. ApprovalScope includes account and environment to prevent transfer.

`matches_current_proposal` checks only supplied contract data. It never grants
execution authority, calls a broker, evaluates economic edge, or replaces an
independent Risk, capital, operational or OMS gate. Its caller must provide
trustworthy current scope and time. Persisted invalidation, recovery, clock
rollback monitoring and authoritative evidence resolution remain later runtime
responsibilities. REJECTED, EXPIRED and INVALIDATED proposals cannot match;
UNKNOWN, PENDING, REJECTED, EXPIRED, REVOKED and SUPERSEDED approvals cannot match.
NO-TRADE exposes no conversion operation and always fails approval matching.

No persistence, external service, strategy, risk engine, OMS, operational mode,
new dependency or lifecycle promotion is included. M1.7 is not started. Historical
bootstrap status and proposed package changes in the older source bundle are
not applied over the user's current certified checkpoint.
